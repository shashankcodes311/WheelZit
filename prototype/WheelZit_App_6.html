Why WheelZit Will Win

₹12,000 crore market. Less than 1% organised. Patent-pending IoT innovation. Built by an ECE founder who lives in NCR.

🎯

The Problem

200 million bikes in India sit idle 18+ hours a day. Students, tourists, and gig workers can't rent a bike for just 2 hours. Daily shops charge ₹800 minimum. No GPS-tracked, KYC-verified, hourly platform exists in NCR.

💡

Our Solution — WheelZit

Asset-light marketplace — like Airbnb for bikes. Owners list idle bikes, riders book by the hour from ₹50. We earn 25% commission. Zero fleet means fast scaling. And our secret weapon: WheelGuard.

⚡

WheelGuard™ — Patent Pending

A ₹1,200 IoT device (ESP32 + SIM800L GSM + GPS) installed under the bike seat. If theft is detected, press one button in the app — the engine cuts off remotely. GPS sends coordinates to police. No rental company in India has built this. Provisional patent filed April 2026.

📈

The Market

Urban mobility growing 18–20% CAGR. No dominant player in hourly 2W rental in NCR. We are the only self-ride + hourly + verified + IoT-secured platform in the region.
